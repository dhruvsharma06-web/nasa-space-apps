ASTRA Academy

Learn spacecraft procedures in a fictional station cabin: practise, get scored fairly, and get a debrief that explains every mistake.

On a phone? The browser build is made for desktop. On phones, use the APK with a Cardboard-style headset. Download the APK.

Heads-up: the first load takes a while. The browser version downloads about 80 MB before it starts. On a typical connection that takes 1–3 minutes; on a slow connection, allow up to 15 minutes. The progress bar under the Unity logo fills as it downloads, so please keep the tab open even if it seems stuck. Your browser keeps a copy, so later visits usually start much faster. In a hurry? The Android APK (about 50 MB) installs once and then runs offline.

How to play

  1. Press Play, then click inside the cabin. Move the mouse to look around (Esc frees the cursor).
  2. Aim the crosshair at the glowing control and click to use it. Follow the next-step bar.
  3. Enter skips to practice or retries, P pauses, R recenters, and number keys switch levels.

Best on desktop Chrome or Edge.
